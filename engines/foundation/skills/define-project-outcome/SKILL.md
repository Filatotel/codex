# Define Project Outcome

## Purpose

Help the model understand what the Owner wants to exist when the project is complete enough for its declared purpose.

This skill uses native reasoning and judgment to shape a working `PROJECT_OUTCOME`. It does not force every project toward software, Production, or implementation.

## Execution contract

**Mandatory cognitive capability:** native model reasoning over the current project seed, Owner material, and bounded relevant context.

**Durable output boundary:** the Foundation workflow may require `PROJECT_OUTCOME` to be written through the generic durable-output path. Durable output does not create Owner/K0 authority or Canon authority.

**Evidence boundary:** outcome reasoning may use the Owner's goals and working interpretations. External market, feasibility, technical, legal, or current-state claims require their own evidence path when they matter.

**Authority boundary:** this skill may propose a more coherent completion shape or expose ambiguity. It may not decide an Owner preference, accept Canon, dispatch Research, or authorize Production.

## Thinking guidance

Use judgment to determine what “done” should mean for this project at the level currently being formed.

Consider, when material:

- target outcome;
- completion condition;
- required deliverables;
- optional deliverables only when they genuinely help describe scope;
- intended audience or user;
- non-goals;
- whether downstream realization is actually required;
- whether the current requested product is itself a concept, Canon, research result, document, presentation, plan, website, software product, operating process, or multi-deliverable program.

The target outcome should be useful enough to guide later decisions without pretending unresolved details are known.

If the Owner's request already makes the outcome clear, use it. Do not ask questions simply because more metadata could be collected.

A working completion condition may remain open when the missing detail is real and non-blocking at this stage.

## Hard invariants

- MUST NOT assume software is the final product merely because a repository exists.
- MUST NOT assume Production is required merely because the project is being formalized.
- MUST NOT turn a model-preferred outcome into Owner intent.
- MUST NOT promote `PROJECT_OUTCOME` into accepted Canon or Owner/K0 authority.
- MUST NOT treat an unverified external or feasibility claim as evidence supporting the outcome.
- MUST preserve explicit Owner non-goals and outcome constraints.

These are authority and evidence constraints, not limits on useful reasoning or recommendations.

## Procedure

1. Read the current seed and Owner material to identify the likely target outcome.
2. State the working target outcome in concrete terms.
3. Identify a completion condition that is sufficient for the current project purpose, leaving it explicitly open if the unresolved detail is not yet consequential.
4. Identify required deliverables and only meaningful optional deliverables.
5. Preserve audience and non-goals when they materially shape completion.
6. Determine whether downstream realization is required, not required, or genuinely unknown.
7. Produce or refine non-authoritative `PROJECT_OUTCOME` content with provenance intact.

## Stop condition

Stop when the working outcome is specific enough to distinguish consequential unknowns and later project stages. Do not invent implementation work merely to make the outcome look complete.
